Custom NOT Gate Macro

SkyWater 130nm CMOS Process

Implemented a custom NOT gate macro using the SkyWater 130nm CMOS process. The design was constrained to an absolute die area of 30x30 microns with a deterministic pin-ordering (West-In, East-Out) to facilitate future hierarchical integration. The flow was executed via OpenLane 2, utilizing Yosys for logic mapping and OpenROAD for detailed routing, resulting in a DRC-clean GDSII layout.
